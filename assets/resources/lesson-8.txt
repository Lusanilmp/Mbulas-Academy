MBULA'S ACADEMY | GRADE 10 | GEOGRAPHY
The water cycle

LEARNING GOAL
Trace water through a system

REVISION NOTES
Solar energy drives evaporation. Water vapour cools and condenses into droplets, which can fall as precipitation. Some water infiltrates the soil and some flows as surface runoff. Plants also return water to the atmosphere through transpiration.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Explain how water moves from a dam to a cloud.
Water evaporates from the dam, rises as water vapour, then cools and condenses into droplets.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Which process changes water vapour into liquid droplets?
1. Evaporation
2. Condensation
3. Infiltration

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
Condensation — Cooling water vapour condenses to form liquid droplets.

KEY TERM
Infiltration: Water entering the soil from the surface.
