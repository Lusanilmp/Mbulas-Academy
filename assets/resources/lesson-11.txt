Speed and velocity

LEARNING GOAL
Separate distance from displacement

REVISION NOTES
Distance is the total path travelled; displacement is change in position with a direction. Average speed = total distance/time. Average velocity = displacement/time. Convert time to seconds and distance to metres for SI units.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Walk 30 m east then 10 m west in 20 s. Find average speed and velocity.
Distance = 40 m, so speed = 2 m/s. Displacement = 20 m east, so velocity = 1 m/s east.

TRY IT YOURSELF
A runner travels 100 m in 20 s. What is average speed?
1. 5 m/s
2. 0.2 m/s
3. 120 m/s

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
5 m/s — Average speed = 100/20 = 5 m/s.

KEY TERM
Displacement: Change in position, including direction.
