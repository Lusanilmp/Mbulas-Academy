Newton’s second law

LEARNING GOAL
Use the resultant force

REVISION NOTES
Newton’s second law states Fnet = ma for constant mass. Draw a free-body diagram and choose a positive direction. Add forces algebraically to find the resultant before calculating acceleration.

WORKED EXAMPLE
A 4 kg object has 20 N right and 8 N left acting on it. Find acceleration.
Choose right positive: Fnet = 20 - 8 = 12 N. a = 12/4 = 3 m/s² right.

TRY IT YOURSELF
A net force of 18 N acts on 3 kg. Find acceleration.
1. 54 m/s²
2. 6 m/s²
3. 15 m/s²

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
6 m/s² — a = Fnet/m = 18/3 = 6 m/s², in the direction of the net force.

KEY TERM
Resultant force: The vector sum of all forces acting on an object.
