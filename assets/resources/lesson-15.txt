Photosynthesis

LEARNING GOAL
Explain energy transfer

REVISION NOTES
Photosynthesis uses light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. Chlorophyll absorbs light. Glucose stores chemical energy. Light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature can affect the rate.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Why does increasing light not always increase photosynthesis?
Another factor, such as carbon dioxide concentration or temperature, can become limiting.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Which gas is released during photosynthesis?
1. Carbon dioxide
2. Oxygen
3. Nitrogen

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
Oxygen — Oxygen is released during photosynthesis.

KEY TERM
Limiting factor: A factor in shortest supply that restricts the rate of a process.
