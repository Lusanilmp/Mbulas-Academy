Development indicators

LEARNING GOAL
Compare evidence fairly

REVISION NOTES
GDP per capita is economic output divided by population. It is an average and may hide inequality. Use economic measures together with health, education and access to services rather than treating a single indicator as the whole picture.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Country A has higher GDP per capita but lower life expectancy than B. What can you conclude?
A has higher average economic output, but this does not prove better health outcomes or equal access to resources.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Why can GDP per capita hide inequality?
1. It is an average
2. It counts only children
3. It measures rainfall

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
It is an average — An average does not show how income and resources are distributed.

KEY TERM
GDP per capita: Economic output per person, calculated as GDP divided by population.
