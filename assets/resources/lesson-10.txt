River gradients

LEARNING GOAL
Calculate and interpret gradient

REVISION NOTES
Gradient is vertical difference divided by horizontal distance. Convert both measurements to the same units. Express 1:n by dividing horizontal distance by vertical difference. A smaller n indicates a steeper slope.

WORKED EXAMPLE
A river drops 100 m over 5 km. Find its gradient as 1:n.
5 km = 5 000 m. Gradient = 100/5 000 = 1/50, written 1:50.

TRY IT YOURSELF
A river drops 50 m over 2 km. What is the gradient?
1. 1:40
2. 1:4
3. 1:400

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
1:40 — 2 km = 2 000 m; 2 000 / 50 = 40, giving 1:40.

KEY TERM
Gradient: The ratio of vertical change to horizontal distance.
