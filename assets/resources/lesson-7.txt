MBULA'S ACADEMY | GRADE 9 | GEOGRAPHY
Contour lines

LEARNING GOAL
Read the shape of the land

REVISION NOTES
Contours join points of equal height above sea level. Closely spaced lines indicate steep slopes; widely spaced lines indicate gentle slopes. The contour interval is the vertical difference between consecutive contour lines.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Contour heights are 100 m, 120 m and 140 m. Find the interval.
The difference between consecutive lines is 20 m.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Which contour spacing usually indicates a steep slope?
1. Wide spacing
2. Close spacing
3. Spacing is irrelevant

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
Close spacing — A large height change over a short horizontal distance creates close contours.

KEY TERM
Contour interval: The vertical height difference between consecutive contour lines.
