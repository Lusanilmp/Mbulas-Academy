MBULA'S ACADEMY | GRADE 10 | LIFE SCIENCES
Cell structure

LEARNING GOAL
Connect structure with function

REVISION NOTES
The nucleus contains genetic material and helps control cell activity. The cell membrane regulates movement into and out of the cell. Mitochondria release usable energy during cellular respiration. Plant cells also have a cellulose cell wall and chloroplasts for photosynthesis.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Why do leaf cells often contain many chloroplasts?
Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll that absorbs light energy for photosynthesis.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Which structure regulates movement into and out of a cell?
1. Cell membrane
2. Cell wall only
3. Nucleus

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
Cell membrane — The selectively permeable cell membrane regulates movement of substances.

KEY TERM
Organelle: A specialised structure within a cell that performs a particular function.
