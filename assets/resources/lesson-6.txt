Map scale

LEARNING GOAL
Convert map distance

REVISION NOTES
A ratio scale compares map distance with ground distance in the same units. At 1:50 000, 1 cm represents 50 000 cm, or 0.5 km. Always show your unit conversion.

WORKED EXAMPLE
A road is 4 cm long on a 1:50 000 map. Find ground distance.
4 × 50 000 = 200 000 cm. Divide by 100 000 cm per km: 2 km.

TRY IT YOURSELF
At 1:50 000, what distance does 6 cm represent?
1. 3 km
2. 30 km
3. 0.3 km

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
3 km — 6 × 50 000 / 100 000 = 3 km.

KEY TERM
Ratio scale: The ratio of a distance on a map to the actual distance in the same units.
