Right-angle trigonometry

LEARNING GOAL
Choose the correct ratio

REVISION NOTES
Identify opposite and adjacent relative to the given angle. The hypotenuse is opposite the right angle. SOH CAH TOA: sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse; cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse; tan θ = opposite/adjacent. Set your calculator to degrees for angles in degrees.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Find sin θ when opposite = 3 and hypotenuse = 5.
sin θ = 3/5 = 0.6. Use inverse sine if you need θ, approximately 36.87°.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Opposite = 6 and adjacent = 8. What is tan θ?
1. 0.75
2. 1.33
3. 0.6

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
0.75 — tan θ = opposite/adjacent = 6/8 = 0.75.

KEY TERM
Hypotenuse: The longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle.
