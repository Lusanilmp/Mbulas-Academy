Momentum and impulse

LEARNING GOAL
Link force and momentum

REVISION NOTES
Momentum is p = mv and has direction. Impulse is Fnet Δt = Δp. In an isolated system, total momentum is conserved. Choose a sign convention and keep it throughout the calculation.

WORKED EXAMPLE
A 2 kg trolley changes velocity from 3 m/s east to 5 m/s east in 1 s. Find average net force.
Δp = 2(5 - 3) = 4 kg·m/s east. Fnet = Δp/Δt = 4 N east.

TRY IT YOURSELF
A 3 kg object travels at 4 m/s east. What is its momentum?
1. 12 kg·m/s east
2. 7 kg·m/s east
3. 1.33 kg·m/s east

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
12 kg·m/s east — p = mv = 3 × 4 = 12 kg·m/s east.

KEY TERM
Impulse: The change in momentum, equal to average net force multiplied by time.
