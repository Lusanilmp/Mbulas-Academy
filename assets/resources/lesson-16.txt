Monohybrid inheritance

LEARNING GOAL
Use a genetic cross

REVISION NOTES
In a simple complete-dominance example, A is dominant and a is recessive. A heterozygote Aa produces A and a gametes. A Punnett square lists possible offspring combinations; probabilities do not guarantee outcomes in a small family.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Cross Aa × Aa and give the genotype ratio.
Gametes A and a from each parent give AA, Aa, Aa, aa. Genotype ratio 1 AA : 2 Aa : 1 aa; phenotype ratio 3 dominant : 1 recessive.

TRY IT YOURSELF
In Aa × Aa, what is the probability of aa?
1. 25%
2. 50%
3. 75%

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
25% — One of four equally likely combinations is aa.

KEY TERM
Heterozygous: Having two different alleles for a particular gene.
