Differential calculus

LEARNING GOAL
Find a tangent gradient

REVISION NOTES
The derivative gives the instantaneous rate of change. For a polynomial term axⁿ, the derivative is anxⁿ⁻¹. The derivative of a constant is zero. A tangent at x = k has gradient f′(k).

WORKED EXAMPLE
Find the tangent to f(x) = x² + 3x at x = 1.
f′(x) = 2x + 3, so m = 5. The point is (1, 4). Thus y - 4 = 5(x - 1), giving y = 5x - 1.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Differentiate f(x) = 3x² - 4x + 7.
1. 6x - 4
2. 3x - 4
3. 6x + 7

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
6x - 4 — Apply the power rule term by term; the constant 7 differentiates to 0.

KEY TERM
Derivative: A function describing the instantaneous rate of change.
