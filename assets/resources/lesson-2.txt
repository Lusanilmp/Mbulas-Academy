MBULA'S ACADEMY | GRADE 9 | MATHEMATICS
Linear equations

LEARNING GOAL
Keep both sides balanced

REVISION NOTES
An equation states that two expressions are equal. Undo addition or subtraction first, then undo multiplication or division. Apply each operation to both sides and check your answer by substitution.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Solve 3x + 7 = 22.
Subtract 7: 3x = 15. Divide by 3: x = 5. Check: 3(5) + 7 = 22.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Solve 2x + 5 = 17.
1. x = 11
2. x = 6
3. x = 24

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
x = 6 — Subtract 5 from both sides, then divide by 2.

KEY TERM
Inverse operation: An operation that undoes another, such as subtraction undoing addition.
