MBULA'S ACADEMY | GRADE 8 | MATHEMATICS
Algebraic expressions

LEARNING GOAL
Combine like terms

REVISION NOTES
A term is a number, a variable, or their product. Like terms have exactly the same variable part. Add their coefficients; do not add their powers.

WORKED EXAMPLE
Simplify 3x + 5x - 2.
3x + 5x - 2 = (3 + 5)x - 2 = 8x - 2.

TRY IT YOURSELF
Simplify 4a + 2a.
1. 6a
2. 6a²
3. 8a

ANSWER AND EXPLANATION
6a — Only the coefficients add: 4 + 2 = 6.

KEY TERM
Like terms: Terms with the same variables raised to the same powers.
